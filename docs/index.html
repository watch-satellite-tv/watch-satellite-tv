How it works

  1. Open the bot — tap the button above or search for @watch_satellite_tv_bot in Telegram and press Start.
  2. Open the Mini App — the live TV app opens right inside Telegram; nothing to install.
  3. Pick a channel — browse by country or search across all feeds, then tap a channel to watch it live.

Frequently asked questions

Is Watch Satellite TV really free?

Yes. It is completely free — no subscription, no trial, no hidden fees. The project is open-source and runs on free Telegram infrastructure.

Do I need a satellite dish?

No. The channels stream over the internet inside Telegram. All you need is an internet connection — no dish, no receiver, no antenna.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone that runs Telegram — iPhone and Android. It also works in Telegram Desktop. Nothing extra to install.

Is it legal?

The app lists publicly available live streams from public IPTV directories (such as iptv-org). It does not host video itself; playback goes directly from your device to the stream source.

Why would a channel not play?

Some streams are only reachable from certain countries, or the broadcaster may have taken them down. Tap “Report it” on the channel and it will be checked — dead channels are removed.
